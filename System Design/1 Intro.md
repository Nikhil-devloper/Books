# System Design Interview – Introduction

## Key Takeaways

- **Objective:** Learn to design scalable systems capable of handling massive traffic, transactions, and data.
- **Interview Focus:** Interviewers evaluate your thought process, problem-solving approach, and communication rather than just the final solution.
- **Think Aloud:** Explain your decisions, assumptions, trade-offs, and potential bottlenecks throughout the interview.
- **Reusable Concepts:** System design relies on a common set of architectural patterns and tools that can be assembled to solve different problems.
- **Practical Experience:** Understanding how real-world systems are built is essential.

## Interview Tips

- Ask clarifying questions before designing.
- Break complex problems into smaller components.
- Think out loud and communicate your approach.
- Explain architectural decisions and trade-offs.
- Identify potential bottlenecks and scalability challenges.

## Core Lesson

**System Design is not about finding one perfect answer. It's about demonstrating how you approach unfamiliar problems and design scalable, reliable solutions.**
